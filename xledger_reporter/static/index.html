Xledger Reporter
Capture a report and email it automatically
Settings Saved
How to use
1. Fill in your Xledger login details.

2. In Menu path, type the menu names you click in Xledger to reach the report, separated by >. For example: Reports > Time Registration

3. If the report has a Run or Search button you need to click to generate the data, enter that button name too.

4. Enter the email addresses of the recipients.

5. Click Run Report & Send Email. A browser window will open, log in to Xledger, click through the menus, capture the report, and send it via Outlook.

Note: Outlook must be open and logged in on this PC.
