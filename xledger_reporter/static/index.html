Xledger Reporter
Capture a report and email it automatically
Settings Saved
How to use
1. Fill in your Xledger login and the URL of the report you want to send.

2. Open the report manually in Xledger, copy the URL from your browser address bar, and paste it into "Report URL".

3. Enter the email addresses of the people who should receive the report.

4. Click Run Report & Send Email. A browser window will open, log in to Xledger, capture the report, and send it via Outlook.

Note: Outlook must be open and logged in on this PC.
